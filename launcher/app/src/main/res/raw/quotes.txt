# The quotes screensaver's quotes (QuoteScene). One quote is four lines, then a blank line:
#   the quote in the language it was written in / its author / the quote in the other language / its author
# The two languages are Arabic and English. A quote written in Arabic starts with its Arabic lines and is
# shown Arabic first; any other starts with its English lines and is shown English first.
# " / " inside a quote is a line break (between the two halves of a verse). Lines that start with # are
# notes: each quote's source is written above it. The screensaver adds the quotation marks itself.
# Only quotes with a source that can be checked belong here, and nothing religious.
